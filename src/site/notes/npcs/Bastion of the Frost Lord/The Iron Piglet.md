#NPCS #constructs

The iron piglet is a remarkable clockwork construct, with excellent olfactory senses. It can understand reasonably complex commands, and can also speak a little.

It likes finding truffles.
It obeys [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Orin Farlight\|Orin Farlight]] and whoever else it's been configured to obey.
