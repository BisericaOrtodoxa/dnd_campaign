# The disappearance of Belma Farlight

Discovered in [[sessions/Session 30\|Session 30]] and settled in [[sessions/Session 31\|Session 31]].
This event is initially framed as a disappearance by [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Orin Farlight\|Orin Farlight]], and later a kidnapping by his younger daughter [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Aramila Farlight\|Aramila Farlight]].
Upon investigating further, [[factions/The Party\|The Party]] reaches [[locations/buildings/Lord Frost's Residence\|Lord Frost's Residence]] and gains audience with the head of the estate [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Lord Frost\|Lord Frost]].
As both he and [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Belma Farlight\|Belma]] explain, this arrangement is a consequence of a bargain Orin struck with Lord Frost.
