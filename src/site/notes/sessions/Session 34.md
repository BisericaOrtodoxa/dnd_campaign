# Session 34
#sessions 

Fresh off of the defeat from the previous session, [[factions/The Party\|The Party]] decides to pursue [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|Seiro]]. 
Upon spotting them leaving after the fight was over, [[players/Emery\|Emery]] managed to catch up and attempt an approach.
Upon realising they've been pursued, [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|Seiro]] turns around, makes physical contact with [[players/Emery\|Emery]] and vanishes. The whole encounter didn't feel quite right.

Resuming their shenanigans, the group starts working towards assembling a phylactery.
First, they go on a quest for knowledge regarding on the nature and composition of such a construct. This search leads them in two places. [[locations/buildings/The University Of Arcane Science and Applied Magics\|The University Of Arcane Science and Applied Magics]] and [[The Grand Seamersian Library \|The Grand Seamersian Library ]].
Inside the university, they make contact with a researcher group that provides them with half the recipe for a [[items/items/Lichnee potion\|Lichnee potion]], and in the library, they find the other half.
Before the group reunited again, [[players/Emery\|Emery]] and [[players/Remy\|Remy]] agree to deliver goods across the city for payment. Despite their efforts and success in getting the guards to not inspect the goods, they were carrying completely legal comic books. 
However, the client they were delivering to sold them pure distillate arsenic at quite a steep price.
A person, a chicken and a frog also got arrested for [[concepts/frog touching\|frog touching]] nearby.

Since it was needed for the potion, [[players/Remy\|Remy]] and a local woman break into an orphanage and steal an infant. [[players/Remy\|Remy]] left behind his accomplice.
In order to secure the spellcasting power needed for the ritual, [[npcs/Wesford related/Semearsia's Court Wizard\|Semearsia's Court Wizard]] is convinced to aid their cause. 

While in a nearby forest, the gang encounters the [[locations/villages/Goblin village\|Goblin village]] [[players/Emery\|Emery]] sold and kidnap 3 goblins. 
With the help of imp drawing his best interpretation of a belladona, the plant necessary for the potion is secured. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] and [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] also sequentially fall in a well in the process.
![image-34.png](/img/user/sessions/image%20files/image-34.png)

Using 2 of the 3 kidnapped goblins, a phase spider is convinced into producing some venom and the victim's blood.
Using the power of wealth, [[players/Imp\|Imp]] buys wyvern venom straight from the source.
