# Phylactery

The process of becoming a [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]] is involved, dangerous, and unique to each would-be [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]]. If the rite succeeds, the [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]]'s soul is bound to a phylactery, a specially prepared magical repository. This relic anchors the [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]]'s spirit to the world and preserves it should the [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]]'s body be destroyed. A [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]] can be slain only if its spirit jar is ruined. As such, a [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]] goes to great lengths to hide and protect its spirit jar.
Spirit jars are typically small, well-made objects that were meaningful to a [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]] in life. 

#### Creation

Making a phylactery is a highly expensive and taxing effort, requiring a high degree of spellcasting ability and very expensive materials. While any object could be chosen to be the [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]]'s jar or phylactery, it must be made of a solid material (and not wood) and be of high-quality craftsmanship. 

First, a prospective [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]] must enchant the object. If this works, it is followed by trapping the soul into the object. This has the effect of binding the caster's soul to the mortal realm, stopping it from going onto the other planes after death. 

Next, the [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]]'s soul enters the jar for the first time, which requires them to drink a highly toxic brew known as a [[items/items/Lichnee potion\|Lichnee potion]].
This experience of being shunted into the phylactery is an exhausting process that causes a loss of life-force and might. Once their soul and vitality are successfully stored in the jar, they can leave and rest for at least a few days to recover.
