# the narrative
#concepts

The string of events that leads from the past to the present, as well the projected outcome of the near and far future.
Core concept, proceeds anything and everything else. 

Despite appearances, the narrative is susceptible to cognition. Meaning that it's not an objective record of things as they happen. Simple(and unanimously accepted) misremembering on the part of [[DM ONLY/concepts/Observers\|Observers]] can change it. By nature, observers can't __purposefully__ bend the narrative in any way.
