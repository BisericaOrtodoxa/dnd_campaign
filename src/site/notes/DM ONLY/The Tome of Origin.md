# The Tome of Origin#items

Anomalous item that presents information to [[factions/The Party\|The Party]]. Initially framed as the writings of the Crystal Frost Cult.
As time goes on, the contents the book presents become more and more unrelated to it's initial purpose. Especially after [[items/items/The Deck of Many Things\|The Deck of Many Things]] wishes.
