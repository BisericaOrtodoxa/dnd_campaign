# Session 41
#sessions 

Upon attempting a return home, [[factions/The Party\|The Party]] makes a pitstop at a nearby village. It appears to be a rudimentary settlement consisting entirely of lizard folk.
Due to some karmic retribution, [[players/Imp\|Imp]] gets struck by lightning and ends up in a wheelchair.
![image-35.png](/img/user/sessions/image%20files/image-35.png)
[[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] is contacted by [[npcs/Flying bastion residents/Man carry box\|Man carry box]] and as a result, the party hurries back home. He writes about [[npcs/Flying bastion residents/Carl\|Carl]] doing increasingly worrying things. 
They arrive home, and they decide to murder the man in his sleep.
His neck spouts forth a thick smoke. It completely fills the room. It gets dark, darker, yet darker. 
As their senses return to them, they find themselves in unfamiliar land. An alleyway that houses an armory, a bakery and a statue of an angel. 
They meet a set of people idling about the place, they call themselves the Heroes of Legend. After a lengthy altercation that involved a Fire elemental, they proceed with the only casualty being said elemental. 
Upon proceeding to the next area, it becomes obvious that the environment repeats, becoming simplified. 
To proceed forth, [[players/Imp\|Imp]] solves a weight distribution puzzle. 
And after all this time, they come forth and try to face me. Only it's far too late.

This concludes my documentation of the second set of observers. Should anybody else come upon it, study the parts only accessible to you.
