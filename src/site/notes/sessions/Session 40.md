# Session 40

[[factions/The Party\|The party]] continues in the rush to save The Holidays.
In their mad dash to get to [[npcs/Misc/Santa\|Santa]], his factory is almost set ablaze. 

On the way to the basement, the party gets distracted by some flying fairies and meets a Jack In The Box, who records their wishes for Santa.
[[players/Imp\|Imp]] manages to get some valuable information from them regarding what lies at the bottom of the factory.
Much like the fairies say, they encounter [[Eldara\|Eldara]] who has captured [[npcs/Misc/Santa\|Santa]] and another woman that nobody bothered to identify,
As it turns out, [[Eldara\|Eldara]] has her victims under mind control and initiates a grueling fight.
After [[players/Emery\|Emery]] almost dying multiple times, [[factions/The Party\|The Party]] ends up victorious and earns permanent membership on The Nice List, which is claimed by [[players/Imp\|Imp]].
