# Lichnee potion

A lichnee potion, also known as a potion of transformation, is a highly poisonous mixture that has to be used as part of a ritual to become a [[DM ONLY/concepts/Lichdom/Lich\|lich]].

When properly prepared, the mixture has a sparkling black liquid with a bluish glow.

### Recipe

The following ingredients had to be mixed together in this specific order under the light of a full moon: 
- 2 drops of pure distillate of arsenic
- 1 drop of pure distillate of belladonna
- 1 quart of blood from a human infant killed by wyvern venom 
- 1 quart of blood from a non-human humanoid killed by a phase spider 
- 1 simmered intact heart from a humanoid killed by a mixture of arsenic and belladonna poisoning
- 1 pint or more of fresh phase spider venom (less than a month old)
- 1 pint or more of fresh wyvern venom (less than two months old)
