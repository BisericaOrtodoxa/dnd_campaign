# Session 38

#sessions

__Eastern monastery, pink forest__

Last session imp touched a crystal and got duplicated. The Crew got transported to a different looking 'park' and realized three monks are shaking their hands. The monks are thanking The Crew for saving them from some attack. The three monks freeze in place after [[players/Remy\|Remy]] asks them about what the attack is. Imp investigated the Phantom Imp. Imp doesn't know himself that well.  

Remy and Imp steal a scroll from a frozen monk. Imp reads the scroll flawlessly. Remy pickpockets a frozen monk and finds obsidian. Imp steals bread. Gorich takes a look around the monastery. Remy finds a statue of a fox with a gem embedded in its forehead. Imp struggles with bread identification. Gorich steals the stoneskin pendant from Imp. Imp hides from humanity. And fails. Gorich puts the stoneskin pendant on a fox statue. It does nothing.  

Imp hides from The Crew. Imp manages to perfectly hide behind Phantom Imp. [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] puts [[items/items/The stoneskin pendant\|the stoneskin pendant]] on a dwarf and the dwarf gets stoned. [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] takes the pendant off and the dwarf un-stones.  

The Crew then ponders for a few minutes on what to do next. [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] and [[players/Remy\|Remy]] investigate a shrine. Gorich offers some Suspicious Leaves to the shrine. Remy offers a horseshoe to the shrine. The vibes get slightly better. Emery and Remy start speaking different languages. Gorich puts on the [[items/items/Wizard Hat of Idiot Strength\|Wizard Hat of Idiot Strength]] and soon after regrets it. 
The Crew realities there's nothing past the monastery entrance. Emery perceives something far in the distance, a familiar figure. [[npcs/Flying bastion residents/Carl\|Carl]] appears from the darkness. Imp jumps off the edge, though it turns out he can normally walk and isn't falling. Imp and C/Karl start speaking in the void (hollow knight reference). The Adventurer's Spirit guides The Crew forward.

__Unknown throne room__

The Crew ends up in a fancy corridor in front of a door. The corridor loops, looking right shows the corridor from the left and vice versa. Imp peeks through the door's keyhole and claims he sees a throne room. Imp enters the throne room.  
    
A figure stands on the other side of the room, next to a window letting lots of sun through. The person begins a monologue about the end of an adventure and the party's incoming death. He then realities The Crew is the wrong party. The Crew and the guy, [[npcs/Misc/Gilgamesh\|Gilgamesh]], discuss the situation. They come to the conclusion The Crew's in a different world. [[npcs/Misc/Gilgamesh\|Gilgamesh]] and The Crew end up confused on how to exit. The Crew moves to a 'land of riches'.

__Land of Riches__

THE UNIVERSE GAINS A NEW DIMENSION. [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]]'s fox becomes three dimensional and ends up being really ugly. The Crew attempts to figure out which door is the real one. The Crew convinces a kobold to go through the 'safe' (left) door. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] goes through the right door and ends up falling 30 feet from the sky. [[players/Remy\|Remy]] uses a ring of breeze to force Imp to fall into the water. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] has to pray then climbs out. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] struggles with a 3rd dimension. The Crew begins to guess which door is safe. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] fails miserably. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] jumps into the endless seas, then climbs back up. He continues failing miserably. Imp finally manages to get through the correct door.

__Floating islands__

The Crew ends up on a floating island. The Crew discovers a rotating cow. [[players/Emery\|Emery]] rides the cow. ([[players/Imp\|Imp]] throws a tantrum). The Crew struggles with the concept of a 3rd dimension. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] reads the runes on a huge stone - "This is where the lost gather". The Crew continues on toward the bard, [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|Seiro]].

The Crew appears inside an eastern home. The Crew proceeds to leave.

__Statue room__
The Crew moves to a room with six statues, with buttons on each of them. Each button changes a statue. The puzzle is solved by  [[players/Emery\|Emery]]. The Crew sees the old members of the party. The Party approaches the [[npcs/Alterham Related/Undead Orc Wife\|undead orc wife]] (from all the way in like [[sessions/Session 03\|session 03]]) and gets teleported.

__[[locations/buildings/The Flying Bastion\|The Flying Bastion]]__

The Crew is moved to the bastion. The 3rd Dimension dissapears. The Crew is perplexed upon meeting CKarl, who's playing a piano and is unaware of the situation. The Crew calls it a day.
