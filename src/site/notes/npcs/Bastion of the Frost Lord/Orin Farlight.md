#NPCS
Sir Orin Farlight is a 50-something human male.
He is short and bald, with a large, purple birthmark on his scalp.
He always dreamed of being the greatest artificer in the world.

- He has two daughters, [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Belma Farlight\|Belma Farlight]] and [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/Aramila Farlight\|Aramila Farlight]].
- Belma went missing, on her fifteenth birthday.
- He has thoroughly searched the estate, and is sure she is not here.
- He has a means of tracking her via one of his devices (see [[npcs/Bastion of the Frost Lord/The Iron Piglet\|The Iron Piglet]]).
- He believes Aramila, who is eight, might know something more, but she has been silent.
