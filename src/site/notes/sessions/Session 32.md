#sessions 
# Session 32
  
After leveling up to **Level 7**, the party returned to  [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/The Witch\|The Witch]]'s house, where [[npcs/Erry Family/Larry\|Larry]] the wolf picked up the scent of [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|the bard]]. They stole eight chairs and damaged her porch before moving on.
The group’s starter town was revealed to be [[locations/cities and towns/Dewhurst\|Dewhurst]] but their journey soon led them to the city of [[locations/cities and towns/Drakmor\|Drakmor]]. There, they learned that the mayor is an actual dragon  who executes criminals himself and can shape-shift into a humanoid form.

Trouble began at the city gates, where the party discovered the long and tedious citizenship process required to enter.
The town seemed to be protected by incredibly rigorous guard placements as well as a magic dispelling effect placed on the outer walls.
Despite this, [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] managed to sneak trough.
[[players/Emery\|Emery]] stole the papers of a random immigrant to gain entry then proceeded to sneak the remaining members trough a bag of holding. 
What followed was the **bombing of a homeless shelter**, then the “heroic” aid in damage control to earn favor and access to the inner city. 
An unfortunate [[npcs/Misc/Tortured Capture Assassin\|Tortured Capture Assassin]] got executed after attempting to sneak in illegally.

As a [[bakery customer\|bakery customer]] told them, every citizen is logged into [[locations/buildings/The Registrar\|The Registrar]]. To find out further info about the whereabouts of [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|Seiro]], [[factions/The Party\|The Party]] aims to enter the building and look trough any entries related to said person.
