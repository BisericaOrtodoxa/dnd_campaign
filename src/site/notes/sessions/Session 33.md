# Session 33

Our party enters the city kn search of the bard so they do some goofy stuff beforehand like: 
- imp trying to give DOGSHIT SWORD 2 to Remy only to realize it was bound to him,
- imp again talking to [[npcs/Erry Family/Larry\|Larry]] the wolf about his family and what they are doing which gives them no useful information
- last but not least Emery trying to carve "67" on the walls only for a polymorph spell to turn him into a chicken for absolutely failing. 

Ultimately, it turns night time and our party decides to stay at a bar. [[npcs/Misc/The Drakmor bartender\|The Drakmor bartender]] (name not known), has a supposedly physical form of a voice next to him that acts as a sidekick. 
They decide to get wine and imp decides to sleep. [[players/Emery\|Emery]] on the other hand, decides to gamble against the bartender in a set of blackjack and wins 20 gold.

[[factions/The Party\|The party]], after waiting for [[players/Imp\|Imp]] to wake up, decides to search [[locations/buildings/The Registrar\|The Registrar]] for more info about the Bard ([[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|Seiro]]). 
The front door is locked and the windows are blocked by something, so they decide to sneak in via a grappling hook by breaking an unprotected window in the upper floor. Previous attempts were made with the [[items/items/eight chairs\|eight chairs]], which unfortunately didn't work and broke 2 chairs beyond repair. 
During their search, they find [[items/handouts/3 labyrinth puzzles\|3 labyrinth puzzles]], [[items/handouts/Seiro's registry page\|Seiro's registry page]] along with a page that has a drawing of her appearance. Which was all they could find. 

After asking the locals about [[npcs/Other Major NPCS/Seiro\|Seiro]], they found out that she also attended some important events here too, much like the crack incident and the [[events/The Greyhawk Insurrection\|The Greyhawk Insurrection]]. 
[[factions/The Party\|The party]] comes to an assumption that the bard attends such important and big events and what better way to do a big event than ask the dragon to for a duel? 
They approach the mayor's house for a duel, the conditions are: if the party wins, they get half the land of the city (proposed by the party) and a statue of imp (proposed by the dragon) if the dragon wins, he gets [[locations/buildings/The Flying Bastion\|The Flying Bastion]]. The duel is accepted by both parties and happens in [[locations/cities and towns/Westford\|Westford]]'s coliseum, resulting in a decisive victory of the mayor. The mayor reasonably flies off to claim the bastion for its own while our party is going through the Kübler-Ross model.
