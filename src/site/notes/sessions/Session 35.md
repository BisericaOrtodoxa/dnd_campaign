# Session 35

After the wyvern encounter, Imp and Melon took time to pray. Imp received no particular blessing, while Melon gained some money(just enough to gamble). Imp then played the lyre for a while before investigating the camp’s tents, where they discovered a piece of azurite. They also examined the infamous [[items/items/DOGSHIT SWORD2\|DOGSHIT SWORD2]].
Later, events took a grim turn. The party killed a child in a gruesome fashion. Gorich slit the child’s throat with the venom-coated DOGSHIT SWORD2, then attempted to heal the wound. [[npcs/The orphan infant\|The orphan infant]] later died in agony, revealed to be unnaturally full of blood.
Imp crafted a surprisingly well-made coffin for the body, though it was adult-sized rather than fitting for an infant. The child was buried, and after a somber rest at the camp, the party eventually moved on.

At the castle, the party visited the court wizard and soon remembered they needed a humanoid heart for their next step. To acquire one, they concocted a poison from water, arsenic, and belladonna to kill the [[npcs/goblin mother\|goblin mother]].
Imp struck the goblin with a plank, knocking her unconscious. As a castle cleaner happened to pass through the room, [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] somehow managed to block their view. “Whatcha got there? A smoothie.”
[[players/Remy\|Remy]] then forced the arsenic-belladonna mixture down the goblin’s throat. Emery found a suitable bowl to hold the heart once removed. The goblin died from the poison, after which her body was stored in the bag of holding.
With their task complete, the party left the castle and set off into the forest to finish the Process.

The party headed into said forest to extract the goblin's heart. [[players/Imp\|Imp]] tried to chop down a tree and failed. [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] extracted the heart and got slightly covered in blood. the heart was simmered. 

Following a trail of smoke, [[factions/The Party\|the party]] came upon a burning house with two orcs weeping outside. At [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]]’s urging, Imp blasted a hole through the wall and rushed into the flames to rescue their trapped daughter. Though singed by the fire, Imp managed to save the orc girl and carry her to safety.
Afterward, Imp offered the orcs a generous supply of planks which they gratefully accepted. He then stored four trees inside [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]]’s bag of holding and gave the orcs twenty gold pieces as further aid.

Back at the castle, the party returned to the court mage. Imp and Emery immediately began arguing over who had stolen more from the witch's house. Despite the bickering and a brief dispute about the proper use of salt, the party managed to brew their potion. The concoction was completed successfully and named **Turbo Instant Death Poison**.
Afterward, the group slept on the benches in a nearby room before setting out to find the court wizard again. When they finally located him, [[players/Remy\|Remy]] presented the king with a barrel of rum. The party then transferred the poison into an empty barrel and threw the cauldron, still coated in traces of Turbo Instant Death Poison, into the moat.

In the city, the party began searching for a carriage. [[players/Gorich\|Gorich]] stopped to look over a notice board, where he found a wanted poster for someone called [[npcs/Crimson Fox\|Crimson Fox]] and a goat named [[Sprinkles\|Sprinkles]]. Several local events were also listed. After helping to catch a runaway egg, Gorich received a basket full of eggs as a reward. The party eventually found a carriage and prepared to continue their journey.

On the road to the city, the party was stopped by a comically long line of ducklings following a mother duck. [[players/Emery\|Emery]] and [[players/Imp\|Imp]] struck up a conversation with the lead duck, while Imp began “helping” to move the ducklings. During the exchange, [[players/Imp\|Imp]] asked the unfortunate question, “Can I fuck?” which was, thankfully, ignored by the ducks.
Further along, the group came across a battlefield strewn with dead orc soldiers and a [[young elf child \|young elf child ]] clutching a knife. Imp moved the bodies aside and spoke with the child, who revealed they were an escaped slave traveling with other runaways. The party decided to take the child with them. Later, tensions rose between [[players/Imp\|Imp]] and [[players/Emery\|Emery]], but the child managed to calm the situation with surprising skill.

The party finally arrived in [[locations/cities and towns/Drakmor\|Drakmor]], leaving the child outside while they handled business within. [[players/Remy\|Remy]] was admitted into the dragon’s office and told to wait, as the mayor would see him soon. Growing impatient, [[players/Remy\|Remy]] woke the dragon in the middle of the night, only to discover that the mayor looked like a bear.
[[players/Remy\|Remy]] and [[players/Emery\|Emery]] began negotiating a deal with the dragon, eventually reclaiming the bastion under the condition that it would be given to the dragon once there was no other successor to inherit it. The discussion concluded with [[players/Remy\|Remy]] barking.
