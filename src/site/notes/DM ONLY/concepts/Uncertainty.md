# Uncertainty
Initially, [[DM ONLY/concepts/the narrative\|the narrative]] was centered around the defeat of the [[DM ONLY/factions/The Crystal Frost Cult\|The Crystal Frost Cult]]. Due to a [[spells/Wish\|Wish]], said organisation was erased. However, the mending of inconsistencies left behind by their disappearance was not part of the [[spells/Wish\|Wish]]. As a result, these faults simply faded into [[DM ONLY/concepts/Uncertainty\|Uncertainty]](can also be referred as ambiguity, vagueness etc etc).
This catalytic event leads to the current shape of [[DM ONLY/concepts/the narrative\|the narrative]] and the central role [[DM ONLY/concepts/Uncertainty\|Uncertainty]] will play. 

Before explicitly witnessing it, existence itself is filled with __potential__(aka uncertainty). The simple act of observation reduces this potential into reality. Similar to how light acts as both wave and particles.
The driving force of the collapse of infinite potential is the act of observation which implies the existence of observers.

__[[DM ONLY/concepts/Observers\|Observers]]__ are beings not of the world, but reside in it nonetheless. This does not pertain to gods or other related entities for they too are of the world.
Despite the role of the [[DM ONLY/concepts/Observers\|Observers]], none of them are or need to be aware of their effects in shaping [[DM ONLY/concepts/the narrative\|the narrative]] and the world around them.

However, that does not result in [[DM ONLY/concepts/Uncertainty\|Uncertainty]] being unknown to those *of* the world.
Should an inhabitant become aware of their [[DM ONLY/concepts/meta-existence\|meta-existence]], they would be able to use [[DM ONLY/concepts/Uncertainty\|Uncertainty]] to their desire(provided they surpass the mental toil that comes with acknowledging it). 
Although, for such an actor to exist in the first place, some aspects about their being must also be established. That is the only real limitation of vagueness.
